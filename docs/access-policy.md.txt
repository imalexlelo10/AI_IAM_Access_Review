# LeloTech Access Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the standard security group membership employees should receive based on their job role. The goal is to follow role-based access control (RBAC) and the principle of least privilege.

## Approved Role-Based Access

| Job Role | Approved Security Group |
|---|---|
| IT Support Specialist | GG_IT_Support |
| HR Specialist | GG_HR_Specialists |
| HR Lead | GG_HR_Specialists |
| Financial Analyst | GG_Finance_Analysts |

## Access Rules

Employees should only be assigned to security groups that are approved for their job role.

Any security group membership that does not match the approved role-based access policy should be flagged for review.

A flagged membership does not automatically mean the access is malicious or unauthorized. Legitimate business exceptions may exist and should be reviewed before access is removed.

Access changes should require human review before remediation is performed.
